Stitch & Thread Studio 🪡

Welcome to my little corner of the web dedicated to the art of embroidery!!!

Essential Supplies

  • Wooden hoops in various sizes
  • DMC embroidery floss (all the colors!)
  • Linen and cotton fabric
  • Sharp needles and snips

Favorite Stitches

  • French Knot: Great for texture and floral centers
  • Satin Stitch: Smooth and shiny fill stitch
  • Backstitch: Clean lines for outlines and text

All About Embroidery Hoops

Wooden Hoops: The classic choice! Great for holding fabric taut while stitching, and they double as a display frame for finished pieces.

Plastic & Flexi Hoops: Excellent for high-tension hold while working on heavy fabrics or detailed patterns.

Decorative Frames: Oval, geometric, or vintage-style frames used specifically to showcase finished embroidery on your wall.
